## 👨🏾‍💻 About Me

I am a Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) professional with 8+ years of experience supporting organizations across healthcare, financial services, federal/DoD, and other highly regulated environments.

I specialize in **cybersecurity risk management, security control assessments, audit readiness, third-party risk, cloud security, vulnerability management, and compliance engineering**.

My work includes translating complex security requirements into practical controls, assessing control effectiveness, identifying risk, developing remediation strategies, and producing audit-ready evidence.

### 🎯 Core Expertise

- **GRC & Cyber Risk** — Risk assessments, risk registers, control testing, remediation, exceptions, KRIs/KPIs
- **Federal & DoD Compliance** — NIST 800-53, NIST 800-171, CMMC, FedRAMP, RMF
- **Cloud Security** — Azure, Azure Government, AWS, identity, RBAC, MFA, encryption, logging
- **Third-Party Risk Management** — Vendor assessments, SOC 2 reviews, due diligence, residual risk
- **Audit & Compliance** — ISO 27001, SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS
- 🤖 **AI Governance & Security** — NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, AI risk assessments and governance
